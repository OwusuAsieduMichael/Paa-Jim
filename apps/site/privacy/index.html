Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Paa Jim app collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to customers, delivery riders and kitchen staff who use the app.

“Paa Jim”, “we” and “us” mean Paa Jim Fast Food (Body Body), Effiduase, Ashanti Region, Ghana. We are responsible for your data under Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

In short: we use your data only to take, cook, deliver and support your orders. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. Your MoMo PIN is never seen or stored by us.

Riders

While a rider is delivering an order, from pickup until they arrive, the app records the rider’s location, including while the app is in the background. We use it to show the customer where their food is and to resolve delivery problems. Location recording stops when the delivery ends or the rider logs out. We also keep the rider’s vehicle details, shift status and delivery history.

Kitchen staff

We keep the name, phone number and photo of kitchen staff, and a record of the orders they accept, reject or prepare.

2. Who sees your data

  • Your rider sees your name, phone number, photo and delivery address for the order they are delivering.
  • You see your rider’s name, phone number, photo, vehicle and live location while they bring your order.
  • The kitchen sees your name, phone number and order.
  • Paa Jim administrators can see orders and accounts to give support, handle refunds and prevent fraud. Every administrator action is logged.

3. How long we keep it

  • Your account (name, phone, photo, addresses, saved MoMo numbers): until you delete your account.
  • Rider location points: deleted after 30 days.
  • Notification tokens: deleted after 90 days without use, or when you log out.
  • Orders and payment records: kept for up to six years for tax, accounting and refund purposes, even after you delete your account.

4. Your rights

Under Act 843 you can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong. You can change your name, photo and addresses yourself in the app;
  • delete your data. See Delete your account;
  • stop using your data for a particular purpose.

You can turn off location and notifications at any time in your phone’s settings. Some features, such as delivery to your current location or order alerts, then stop working.

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to Ghana’s Data Protection Commission.

5. Security

Data travels encrypted between the app and our servers. Each person can only see the data their role needs: customers see their own orders, riders see only the deliveries they are doing. Administrators log in with a second verification step.

6. Children

Paa Jim is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. MoMo payments need an account holder’s approval.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is important, we will tell you in the app.
