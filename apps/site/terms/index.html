1. Your account

  • You log in with your Ghana mobile number and a one-time SMS code. Keep your phone and codes to yourself. Orders placed from your account are treated as yours.
  • Give your real name, a phone number you can be reached on and an accurate delivery address.
  • We may suspend an account used for fraud, abuse of riders or staff, or repeated fake orders.

2. Ordering

  • Prices are in Ghana cedis (GHS) and are shown in the app before you pay. The total includes the food, any drinks and the delivery fee.
  • We take orders while the kitchen is open, usually 10:00 to 19:00. The kitchen may close early or pause new orders when it is busy or sold out.
  • Photos are a guide. Portions and presentation can vary a little.
  • Your order is sent to the kitchen once your payment is confirmed.

3. Delivery

  • Delivery times shown in the app are estimates. Traffic, weather and busy periods can make them longer.
  • Please be available on your phone while your order is on the way. Your rider may call you to find you.
  • If the rider cannot reach you or find the address after trying, the delivery may be marked as failed. Contact us and we will sort it out with you.

4. Payment

  • You pay with Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash or AirtelTigo Money) through our payment partner, Paystack. You approve each payment on your phone with your own PIN. We never see or store your PIN.
  • If a payment fails or you do not approve it, no order is placed.

5. Cancellations and refunds

  • If the kitchen cannot take your order, or does not accept it in time, the order is cancelled and you get a full refund to the MoMo number you paid with.
  • Once the kitchen accepts your order, it starts cooking, so you cannot cancel it in the app. If something is wrong, contact us straight away.
  • If an item is missing, wrong or not fit to eat, tell us within 24 hours with your order number, and a photo if you can. We will offer a replacement or a full or partial refund.
  • Refunds go back to the MoMo number you paid with. They usually arrive within a few working days, depending on Paystack and your network.
